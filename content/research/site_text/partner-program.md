Frequently Asked Questions About Partner Program
What is the RAMS Partner Program?
The RAMS Partner Program is a collaborative initiative designed to help businesses, third-party inspectors, and solution providers expand their reach and revenue by leveraging RAMS’ innovative racking management platform. Partners gain access to advanced tools, exclusive resources, and a growing network of warehouse clients.
How does the free demo work?
The free demo offers potential partners a hands-on experience with the RAMS platform. It showcases how RAMS enhances racking inspections, streamlines operations, and improves safety. The demo is tailored to your specific needs and includes a guided walkthrough of its features and benefits.
What kind of Contact does RAMS offer to partners?
RAMS provides comprehensive support to partners, including technical assistance, onboarding guidance, training resources, and a dedicated support team to address any queries. Partners also gain access to marketing materials and tools to promote their services effectively.
Is there a certification process for becoming a third-party inspector?
Yes, RAMS offers a comprehensive certification process for third-party inspectors. This includes specialized training in using the RAMS platform, understanding safety compliance standards, and conducting racking system audits. Certified inspectors gain credibility and access to exclusive business opportunities within the RAMS network.
Are there different partnership levels?
Yes, the RAMS Partner Program offers multiple levels tailored to different business needs. These levels include options for independent inspectors, enterprise-level partnerships, and solution providers, each with varying benefits and access to resources.
How do I get started with the RAMS Partner Program?
Getting started is simple. Visit the RAMS website and fill out the partnership application form or schedule a consultation with our team. Once enrolled, you’ll receive the necessary resources and guidance to begin leveraging the RAMS platform for your business success
