What is RAMS?
RAMS (Rack Asset Management System) is an innovative solution designed to Enhance Warehouse Safety, Efficiency, and Compliance. It utilizes Digital Twin technology, IoT-based monitoring, and advanced diagnostic tools, combined with domain knowledge in racking systems, to address real-world challenges in warehouse operations.
Our Story
Crafted out to bridge the gap between traditional warehouse practices and the modern demands of safety, efficiency, and compliance. With our roots in Built Environment, we recognized the lack of digitization and proactive solutions in racking systems. Our story is one of transformation—creating smarter, more efficient warehouses while addressing real-world challenges with precision and trust.
