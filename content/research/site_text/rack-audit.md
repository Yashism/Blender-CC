When Is The Right Time For A Rack Audit?
Whether it’s a warehouse audit, pallet racking audit, or rack inspection checklist, knowing when to
assess your racking systems prevents accidents and ensures compliance.
Post-Collision or Visible Rack Damage
One impact can compromise an entire structure. Waiting increases the risk of collapse, injury, orinventory loss.
Every 12 Months (At Minimum)
Regulations and best practices require annual audits. Skipping them? You’re risking fines, downtime—and worse.
Before Stability Certificate Expiry
Renewals aren’t rubber stamps. Only a proper audit ensures you pass without costly delays.
After New Rack Installation
Ensure racks are installed as per standards and issue a stability certificate before usage begins.
Before Reinstallation of Used Racks
Racks that are dismantled and reused across sites (or sourced from another warehouse) must undergo a detailed audit.
When Safe Working Loads Are Unknown
You're operating in the dark—posing serious risks to safety, compliance, and liability
After Every Layout Change
New configurations? New risks. Auditing helps ensure stability and compliance after warehouse changes.
After a Natural Disaster
Events like earthquakes, floods, or cyclones can subtly weaken rack foundations and joints.
Before Insurance Renewal / Claims Submission
Ensure you're compliant with insurer safety requirements and avoid claim rejections by performing a formal rack audit beforehand.
