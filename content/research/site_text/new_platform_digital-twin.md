THE RAMS 8D FRAMEWORK
Eight connected layers
Of understanding.
A 3D model answers the first two. Everything after that is what makes it a twin.
01
Spatial
Where is it?
Geometry, layout, dimensions, zones and the spatial relationships between everything in the building.
02
Asset
What is it?
Class, specification, make and the identity that follows the asset from installation to disposal.
03
Time
How has it changed?
Every state the facility has been in, retained — so change is measurable rather than remembered.
04
Condition
What condition is it in?
Structural and operational state against the standard it was designed to, with the severity that follows.
05
Operation
How is it being used?
Occupancy, throughput, the traffic that passes it and the equipment that works it.
06
Simulation
What if we change it?
Reach, clearance, flow and capacity, answered against the model while the change is still a proposal.
07
Live
What is happening now?
The current reading, in place — impact, tilt, load, movement and the events arriving this second.
08
Intelligence
What does it mean next?
The seven layers above read together: a pattern rather than an alert, and what to do before it repeats.
