ABC INVENTORY ANALYSIS
Focus attention
where value matters most.
Classify inventory into A, B and C categories using consumption value, movement and business criticality — so teams apply the right level of control, cycle counting and replenishment attention.
A
TIER 1 · HIGHEST PRIORITY
CONTROL INTENSITY
High-value / Critical Inventory
Items requiring the strongest visibility and tighter operational control because they contribute the highest value or business impact.
Higher cycle-count frequency
Stricter location accuracy
Closer aging & exception monitoring
B
TIER 2 · BALANCED CONTROL
CONTROL INTENSITY
Moderate-value Inventory
Items that need balanced control — regular verification and replenishment visibility without the same intensity as A-class inventory.
Periodic cycle counting
Movement & dwell-time monitoring
Exception-based review
C
TIER 3 · SIMPLIFIED CONTROL
CONTROL INTENSITY
Lower-value / High-volume Inventory
Items that may represent a larger number of SKUs but comparatively lower inventory value, allowing simpler control policies.
Simplified counting strategy
Bulk movement visibility
Space & replenishment optimisation

into smarter warehouse decisions.
ABC analysis tells you which inventory deserves the most attention. When that priority data is combined with ATOS movement intelligence, RAMS can show how pallets actually move through the warehouse — and where travel, handling and storage decisions can be improved.
FROM CLASSIFICATION TO OPTIMISATION
Know which pallets matter — and how efficiently they are being handled.
ATOS adds the movement layer to ABC analysis: how often a pallet moves, where it travels and how efficiently it is stored and handled.

WHAT ATOS UNLOCKS
01
Measure pallet movement frequency
See how many times each pallet is moved over a day, shift or selected period. High-frequency movement can reveal fast-moving inventory, unnecessary re-handling and avoidable touchpoints.
02
Understand where every pallet moves
Track the sequence of locations a pallet passes through during the day — from storage to staging, picking, dispatch or another rack position — to identify inefficient movement patterns.
03
Smarter Storage Height Decisions
Frequently accessed A-class inventory can be evaluated for lower, faster-to-reach storage positions, while slower-moving stock can be considered for higher levels — supporting safer and more efficient slotting decisions.
