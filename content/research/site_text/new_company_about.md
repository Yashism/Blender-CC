TRUSTED ON THE WAREHOUSE FLOOR
WHY RAMS EXISTS
Physical operations generate data
Very little of it has context.
Facilities are managed through disconnected drawings, inspections, sensors, spreadsheets, systems and personal knowledge.
The model becomes outdated
Drawings and layouts rarely preserve every change made after installation.
Assets lose their operating history
Condition, movement, maintenance and documents separate from the physical asset.
Systems explain fragments
WMS, ERP, sensors and reports each see part of the operation — not the whole context.
Decisions arrive after reality changes
By the time information moves through calls, reports and presentations, reality may have changed.
OUR STORY
Built from the
Ground reality up.
RAMS did not begin with a generic dashboard. It grew from hands-on engineering and inspection work, where physical assets, operational risks and corrective actions were still managed through static records.
01 · FIELD EXPERIENCE
Understand the physical problem
Engineering assessments and warehouse inspections revealed how quickly asset condition and operating reality separate from drawings and reports.
02 · DIGITAL ORGANISATION
Create persistent asset identity
Facilities and physical assets became digitally structured, tagged and connected to inspection, action and lifecycle records.
03 · LIVE CONNECTION
Bring operations into the model
Sensor, MHE, inventory, safety and enterprise data added a live layer to the physical context.
04 · PHYSICAL OPERATING SYSTEM
Build applications around reality
RAMS evolved into a platform where operational applications and customer-specific workflows run against the same Digital Twin foundation.
WHAT WE BUILT
The operating context
For the physical world.
RAMS Digital creates a persistent connection between the facility, its assets, their lifecycle and the work happening around them.
01 · PHYSICAL WORLD
Start with what is actually there
People, assets, machines, inventory, infrastructure
The building and everything working in it. Every other layer here is an abstraction of this one, and is only worth anything while it still matches.
02 · EDGE + IOT
Read the building as it works
Coverage depends on the hardware and the site
LiDAR, sensors, cameras, machines and supported customer hardware, reporting position, condition and event as they happen rather than at the end of a shift.
03 · DIGITAL TWIN
Hold the physical truth
The layer everything above it stands on
Physical context, asset identity and lifecycle history, kept current — so an asset still answers the same questions months later, and every reading has somewhere to belong.
04 · AI + ANALYTICS
Find what separate systems miss
Evidence and source stay visible
Vision, spatial analysis, pattern recognition and decision support, applied to what the floor reports rather than to a summary of it.
05 · PROCESS APPLICATIONS
Run the work in context
Each stands alone, and connects
Safety, productivity, inventory, maintenance, inspection and execution, every one of them running against the same physical record.
06 · BUSINESS SYSTEMS
Connect without erasing the source
Read, and write back where it is approved
ERP, WMS, MES, CMMS, TMS and customer platforms, connected so operational reality reaches them — and so their context reaches the floor.

WHERE WE WORK
Built for environments
Where physical operations matter.
The Digital Twin foundation can extend beyond warehousing wherever assets, infrastructure and live operations require persistent context.
Warehouses
Storage structures, handling equipment, inventory and the people moving between them.
Distribution centres
High-throughput sites where receiving, staging, put-away and despatch compete for the same floor.
Factories
Production lines, material flow and the equipment that has to keep running between them.
Industrial plants
Process equipment and fixed installations, where condition and access are governed by procedure.
Logistics facilities
Cross-dock, yard and transfer operations, where assets move between owners and systems.
Utilities
Distributed assets across a network, inspected on a cycle rather than watched continuously.
Infrastructure
Long-lived built assets, where the condition history matters more than any single survey.
Large built environments
Campuses, terminals and complexes where several operations share one physical estate.
