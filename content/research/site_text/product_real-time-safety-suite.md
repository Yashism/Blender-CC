What is RTSS?
The Real-Time Safety Suite (RTSS) is a critical component of RAMS, designed to monitor and improve safety across warehouse operations. By integrating real-time tracking, impact detection, and behavior analysis, RTSS helps identify potential safety risks before they escalate. With automated alerts and preventive measures, RTSS empowers warehouse managers to ensure a safe working environment while minimizing downtime and reducing accident-related costs.

Frequently Asked Questions About RTSS
How does RTSS detect impact events in real time?
RTSS uses advanced sensors installed on racks and MHEs to detect impact events as they occur. These sensors trigger immediate alerts, enabling swift responses to potential damage or safety breaches.
Can RTSS integrate with our existing safety protocols?
Yes, RTSS seamlessly integrates with existing safety protocols by providing real-time data, alerts, and detailed logs that align with your organization’s compliance and safety standards. This ensures an enhanced and unified safety management system.
How does RTSS improve driver behavior and accountability?
RTSS monitors driver actions such as impacts, sudden braking, and acceleration patterns. It generates performance scores and detailed reports, fostering accountability and encouraging safe driving practices through data-driven feedback.
What kind of data does RTSS collect and analyze?
RTSS collects data on impact events, driver performance, MHE usage patterns, and rack structural conditions. This data is analyzed to provide actionable insights for improving safety, reducing accidents, and ensuring compliance.
