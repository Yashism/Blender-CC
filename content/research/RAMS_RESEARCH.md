## 3. What we sell

### 3a. Services (today's revenue wedge)

**Rack Audit & Inspection.** The main call to action on the live site is "Book a Rack Audit". The offer: **Stability Certificate + 3 months of free RAMS software**.

| Package | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Essential Rack Audit** | One-time compliance | Digital Twin creation, EN 15635 inspection, QR codes, RAG (Red-Amber-Green) categorisation, vertical alignment test, beam deflection test, standard reports, BOQ, 3 months software, preventive maintenance plan |
| **Performance Rack Audit** | Ongoing programme | All of the Essential audit, **plus an Annual Maintenance Contract** |
| **Advanced Rack Audit** | Structural analysis | All of the Essential audit, plus thickness measurement, **FEM analysis**, **Safe Working Load (SWL) calculation**, utilisation and deflection ratios |

When to audit (these are the buying triggers):
- after a collision
- every 12 months
- before the stability certificate expires
- after a new installation
- before reusing old racks
- when the safe working load is unknown
- after a layout change
- after a natural disaster
- **before insurance renewal or a claim**

Other services on the new site:
- engineering assessments
- forensic investigations
- digital-twin rollouts
- MHE safety

**Partner Program:** certified third-party inspectors (training, certification, a badge, priority jobs, a partner directory, marketing kits). It's a supply-side network.

### 3b. Software platform (applications on one Digital Twin)

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Digital Twin** (the "8D" framework) | Facility model built from DWG/LiDAR/point cloud. 8 layers: spatial, asset, time, condition, operation, simulation, live, intelligence. Integrates ERP/WMS/MES/CMMS/BMS, and MQTT/OPC-UA/Modbus/BACnet. |
| **IRDS**: Integrated Rack Diagnostic Suite | Digital audits, RAG tagging, OEM-specific BOQs, layout heat map of issues, internal vs external audit comparison |
| **MEPS**: Mobile Equipment Positioning Suite | Live MHE tracking, utilisation, idle time, routes, operator access |
| **RTSS**: Real-Time Safety Suite | Impact detection (rack and MHE sensors), driver behaviour, speed and zone alerts |
| **IMDS**: MHE diagnostics | Condition, faults, battery health, maintenance |
| **IBIS / IROS**: Inventory intelligence | Location visibility, reconciliation (phantom and misplaced stock), aging, ABC analysis, drone capture |
| **ATOS**: Task orchestration | Dynamic task assignment, re-planning (e.g. a delayed truck), exception management |
| **AIMS**: Management intelligence | Multi-site control tower, operations index, cross-module insights, action closure |
| Custom apps | Built on the same Digital Twin |

The adoption path the site pushes: **Model → Tag → Connect → Operate → Learn → Evolve**. Start with Digital Twin + IRDS (racks), then add modules.

## 5. Standards and regulation we can teach (authority content)

- **EN 15635**: inspections (daily/weekly visual, monthly formal, annual expert), **PRSES** role, RAG damage classification (Green: monitor; Amber: repair within 4 weeks; Red: unload now), record-keeping, no improper repairs.
- **EN 15512 / EN 15620**: design and tolerances. OSHA guidance.
- **Factories Act 1948**: employer responsible for safety of infrastructure.
- Insurers increasingly ask for documented audits before paying claims.
- "Stability Certificate" myths: it is a snapshot on one date, **not a guarantee**.
- Gati Shakti compliance guide (downloadable on the live site).

## 7. Gaps and risks a buyer would notice

1. **Two websites, two stories.** The live site says "Rack Asset Management System"; the new one says "Operating System for Intelligent Facilities". Every link we post must land on one consistent story.
2. **No published proof.** Case studies are "Coming soon", so every claim on social is unbacked. This is the #1 gap.
3. **Conflicting numbers:** 300+ vs 200+ sites, 23+ vs 20+ states. Pick one set and use it everywhere.
4. **Hardware pages say "Coming soon"** (AI Cameras, OmniBox, LiDAR, Driver Monitoring). Don't send paid or organic traffic to them until they're live. Use a dedicated landing page or a demo form instead.
5. **Mis-routed pages on the new site:** `/platform/security` shows AIMS content, and `/platform/ai-operational-intelligence` shows ATOS content.
6. **Unsourced performance numbers** on the MHE page. Legal and credibility risk if posted as fact.
7. **Jargon.** "8D", "operating context", "physical operating system" mean little to a plant head. Social should lead with the problem in plain words.
