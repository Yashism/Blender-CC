# RAMS: what we push to the internet (next 90 days)

Built on `content/research/RAMS_RESEARCH.md`. Written from the seat of the person who owns pipeline, not likes.

## The hard truth first

1. **We get paid for rack audits today.** The AI cameras, OmniBox, LiDAR and the Digital Twin are the future and the halo, but the audit is how a stranger becomes a customer. An audit gives us a stability certificate, a Digital Twin of the site and 3 free months of software: a foot in the door for every module we sell later. So **most of our content must create audit demand**, and the flashy product films must *pull people into* that funnel, not sit beside it.
2. **We have no public proof.** 85+ clients, Myntra and Flipkart on the logo wall, 3M+ sq ft inspected, and not one case study online. That is our biggest marketing problem and our biggest opportunity: the proof already exists, it just isn't published.
3. **Buyers don't buy "an operating system for intelligent facilities".** A plant head buys "my racks won't fall", "my insurer will pay", "my client's audit will pass", "my forklifts won't hit anyone". Talk in their fear and their KPI, never in our architecture.
4. **People follow people, not logos.** On LinkedIn the company page gets a fraction of the reach of a founder or a Chartered Engineer posting from the field. Our engineers on site are our best media channel.

## The content engine: 5 series, each with a job

### 1. "Red Tag Files": real findings from real audits (the hero series)
- **What:** One finding per post from our field work, anonymised: a photo of a bent upright, a missing beam lock, an overloaded bay. Each covers what it is, why it's dangerous, the EN 15635 tag (Red/Amber/Green) and the fix.
- **Why:** Nobody else has thousands of bays of field evidence. It's authentic, it's scary, it's useful, and it's shareable. Every safety head reposts it to their team.
- **Format:** Phone photo or 15 s clip from site plus a 3-panel overlay (*Found → Risk → Fix*), or a 5-slide carousel. Posted by the engineer who found it, reshared by the page.
- **Cadence:** 2 per week. This is our weekly heartbeat.
- **Hooks:**
  - "This upright passed a visual check. It was carrying 4 tonnes."
  - "A 3 mm dent is Green. This is not 3 mm."
  - "Your stability certificate is dated. Your forklifts aren't."

### 2. "Certified ≠ Safe": the education and compliance series (authority + SEO)
- **What:** Plain-language explainers:
  - EN 15635 in 60 seconds
  - What a stability certificate does and doesn't prove
  - Who is liable when a rack falls (operator vs OEM vs inspector)
  - Who your PRSES is
  - Safe working load
  - The insurance-claim checklist
  - When to audit (our 9 triggers)
- **Why:** It ranks on Google (rack audit, rack inspection, stability certificate, EN 15635 India) and makes our engineers the people the industry asks. Each piece ends with "Book an audit".
- **Format:** LinkedIn carousels (PDF document posts), a blog article each, a 45 s talking-head by our Chartered Engineer, and a downloadable checklist as a **lead magnet**.
- **Cadence:** 1 per week.

### 3. "See it happen": the product films and clips (the halo)
- **What:** The films we've already built, cut for social:
  - **OmniBox AI**: the 60 s film, plus six 10–15 s use-case clips (PPE, danger zone, forklift near-miss, fall, fire + headcount, idle docks), square and vertical.
  - **AI Camera + OmniBox Edge**: the 360° forklift stop: "the forklift stops before it reaches the person".
  - **LiDAR RTLS**: "Every move leaves a line" (tracking, traces, heat map), as the 65 s film plus 3 clips.
  - **Digital Twin / AIMS**: a 30 s screen-and-3D walkthrough: "Open any site from one screen".
- **Why:** It makes a 2-person safety problem look like a modern technology company: it wins attention, recall and investor and enterprise credibility. But **each clip must end on the problem it solves and a demo CTA**, not on the tech.
- **Cadence:** 1 per week, more around launches.

### 4. "Proof": customers and numbers (closes deals)
- **What:** Case studies (from Fix first), short customer quotes on video, before/after numbers, "site went live" posts, audit milestones ("1,000th red tag closed this quarter").
- **Format:** Carousel case study (*Problem → What we found → What changed → Number*), 30 s customer clip, quote card.
- **Cadence:** 2 per month, rising as we collect more.

### 5. "Bolt's Night Shift": the safety culture series (reach + goodwill)
- **What:** The animated felt-and-cardboard safety shorts we've already built (Ep. 5 "Blind Corner" exists in 16:9 and 9:16). One rule per episode: blind corners, overloading, speed in aisles, PPE.
- **Why:** Shop-floor safety content gets shared *into* companies. Safety managers put it on their TV screens and in toolbox talks. It builds the RAMS name with the people who actually work the floor, and it's the only series that can travel on Instagram, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp.
- **Offer:** "Free for your toolbox talk: download the episode." Gate it with a light email form for leads.
- **Cadence:** 1 episode per month (Ep. 5 first).

## Signature campaigns (bigger moves)

### A. "The India Rack Safety Report 2026" (the flagship asset)
- **What:** Aggregated, anonymised data from our inspections: 300+ warehouses, 3M+ sq ft, thousands of bays.
  - % of bays with Red/Amber damage
  - the 10 most common defects
  - damage by industry and rack age
  - how many sites have an expired or no stability certificate
  - average time from finding to repair
- **Why:** Nobody in India has this data. It's **press-worthy**, gives sales a reason to call, and positions RAMS as *the* authority. One report feeds three months of posts.
- **Rollout:**
  - a gated PDF
  - a launch webinar with an insurer or rack OEM
  - a press release to logistics and supply-chain media
  - 10 carousels, each one a finding
  - a founder post series
  - a stand-up talk at a trade show
- **Owner:** the engineering team pulls the data; marketing designs. Target: week 6.

### B. OmniBox AI + AI Vision launch ("Your cameras record everything. Nobody is watching.")
- **Teaser (−7 days):** one dark 6 s clip. CCTV wall, "Nobody is watching", a date.
- **Launch day:**
  - the 60 s film on the company page, plus a founder post with a personal story ("why we built it to run without the internet")
  - the landing page
  - a demo form
- **Week 1–3:** the six use-case clips (two a week) and the teardown carousel ("What's inside a box that watches 24 cameras": NVIDIA Jetson, on-site).
- **Week 2:** a 30-min **live demo webinar**: "AI on your existing CCTV, no cloud". Invite IT and EHS together, because IT blocks cloud video and EHS wants detections. "No cloud" is the unlock for both.
- **Sales follow-up:** every webinar attendee gets the clip that matches their industry.

### C. "Insurance-ready warehouse" partnership
- **What:** Co-branded content and an offer with one insurer or broker: "Audited sites get faster claims / better terms". A checklist, a joint webinar, a co-signed carousel.
- **Why:** Insurance renewal is our sharpest buying trigger. A broker sharing our audit to their book of clients is a channel, not a post.

### D. Rack OEM and MHE dealer channel
- **What:** "Certified installation" content with rack OEMs: an audit at handover plus the Digital Twin. "Fleet safety pack" with forklift dealers: the AI Camera + OmniBox Edge on new trucks.
- **Why:** They meet our buyer before we do. Content that makes *them* look good gets shared to *their* customers.

### E. Account-based push: Top 100 targets
- **Who:** A named list of 3PLs, e-commerce and fulfilment, auto, FMCG and pharma warehouses, especially multi-site.
- **What they get:** tailored LinkedIn ads (matched audiences), connection and engagement by our founders and engineers, and a personalised "rack risk snapshot" offer (a 1-page desk review from their public site and photos).
- **Measure:** meetings booked per account.

## What we measure (and what we ignore)

**North star:** **rack audits booked** and **demos booked** that came from content.
- **Leading indicators:**
  - audit-page visits and form fills
  - lead-gen form submissions (report, checklist, webinar)
  - webinar attendance
  - follower growth **within our target titles**
  - engagement rate on founder posts
- **Pipeline:**
  - MQL → meeting → audit
  - cost per audit lead
  - audits that convert to a software subscription
- **Ignore:** vanity reach and likes that don't come from our audience.

## Rules for every post

1. **Problem first, product second, CTA last.** "Book a rack audit" or "Book a demo", every time.
2. **Plain words.** No "8D", "operating context" or "physical OS" in social hooks.
3. **One claim, one proof.** If we can't back a number, we don't post it.
4. **Faces from the field beat renders from the studio**, but renders make us look like a category leader. Use both.
5. **Square or vertical, captions burned in, hook in the first 2 seconds.**
6. **Anonymise every client site** unless we have written permission.
