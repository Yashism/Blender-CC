# "See Further": image prompts for mood frames

These prompts are for any text-to-image tool: Midjourney, Flux, Imagen, GPT-image, Higgsfield and so on. Shot numbers match `TREATMENT.md`.

**Tips**
- Attach `assets/refs/rams_ai_camera_ref.webp` as an image or style reference wherever the tool allows it. It keeps the product shape right.
- Generators mangle logos. Treat these frames as mood only; the film uses the real logo file.
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (Midjourney: `--ar 16:9 --style raw`).
- Negative prompt, where the tool supports one: `cartoon, illustration, toy, plastic look, text, watermark, extra lenses, distorted logo, lowres, oversaturated`.

**Product description.** Paste this wherever a prompt says `[CAMERA]`:

> a compact rectangular industrial AI camera about 16 cm tall, matte black anodised aluminium housing with fine diagonal ribbed grooves on the sides and softly rounded vertical edges, a white painted aluminium front faceplate held by four silver cross-head screws at the corners, a small "RAMS DIGITAL" wordmark with an orange corner bracket on the upper faceplate, one round black lens low and centred on the faceplate, a thin bright orange frame around a black rubber pad on the top face, a USB-C port and a yellow XT30 power connector on the side
