# RAMS AI Camera film: v4 review and v5 direction

## How an Apple-commercial cinematographer would rate v4: about 5/10

**What works:** it uses the real product (CAD), the structure follows the music, there's a clear beat for every feature, the type sits behind the product, and the 24/7 day-night cycle and the exploded internals are strong ideas.

**What breaks the premium feel:**

1. **Too much "edit".** Flash frames, camera shake, light leaks, whip blur and punch-in words on nearly every cut. Premium films are restrained: most cuts are invisible, and the effects are light and focus.
2. **Repetition.** The feature montage repeated all six words, the beat cuts reused angles already seen, and the end card listed the specs again. Each idea should be said once.
3. **Generic camera language.** Orbit, push, orbit, push. There was little variation in lens, height, speed or motion type (crane, truck, fly-through, tumble, focus pull).
4. **Unmotivated transitions.** Shots were cut, not connected. Apple connects shots through light (a highlight that wipes the frame), shape (a circle into a circle), motion (a cut on a matching move) and "through" moves (into the lens, out of the lens).
5. **The logo ending wasn't connected to the film.** It was a flat line drawn on black.

## v5 rules

- Every shot is **one idea**. Every feature appears **once**.
- Transitions: invisible cuts on motion, **match cuts**, **soft dissolves** (real overlapping frames, rendered as "handles"), **light wipes** and one **through-the-lens** move. No flash frames, no shake, no light leaks.
- The type is one line, lower-left, Inter Display, fading up. The reveal is the only exception: "AI | Camera" flanks the product, behind it.
- The camera never repeats a move type back to back.
