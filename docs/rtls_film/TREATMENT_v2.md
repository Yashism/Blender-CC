# RAMS LiDAR film, v2: "Every move leaves a line"

A ground-up redesign after the v1 animatic. v1 cut between four looks (daylight model, cyan point cloud, white
clay twin, cutaway diorama), watched everything from far away in locked isometric frames, and used 19 tiny
shots with captions pasted on dark scrims. v2 has one idea, real cameras and fewer, longer shots.

## The idea

The film is told through the forklift's line.

1. **Outside**, a GPS ring follows MHE-07 across the night yard. At the door, the ring breaks.
2. **Inside**, eight forklifts move, and nobody can say where. A rack end takes a knock, and nobody knows who.
3. **The LiDAR wakes up** on MHE-07 (the product moment).
4. **The warehouse becomes dots**, like the RAMS website: every rack, pallet and the floor rebuilt as an even
   lattice of teal dots, painted in by a rotating radar beam on its first turn. Then the orange ring closes on
   the truck: within 1 cm.
5. **Every move leaves a line.** Back in the real warehouse, MHE-07 drives and leaves a line of RAMS orange. The
   camera rises, and the other seven forklifts' lines appear in warm white.
6. **A whole shift, one picture.** From directly above, the lines of a two-hour shift weave together and turn
   into the heat map (blue = quiet, red = busy), with call-outs: the busiest aisle, where forklifts queue, the
   rack hit.
7. **It's the Digital Twin.** The map shrinks into the RAMS Digital Twin app window with the live fleet list.
8. **Start with one aisle.** Logo, Book a demo.

## The look

- **The warehouse under its own lights**: warm high-bays and a cool fill from the skylights, seen from inside
  with the roof on, so the outside never shows (except the night yard in the hook).
- **One accent colour**: RAMS orange only for the hero line, the lock ring, the accent word in each line and the
  call to action. The other lines are warm white. The forklifts are safety yellow, so they no longer fight the
  brand orange.
- **Two worlds, clearly separated**: the real warehouse, and the website's dot world for the LiDAR's view. The
  heat-map colours appear only in the map scene.
- **Cameras**: dollies, cranes and orbits with shallow focus in perspective; flat top-down views for the map.
- **Type**: Outfit, big lines revealed word by word, one orange accent word, no boxes behind the words. Thin
  leader-line labels for the trucks and call-outs. A soft bloom and vignette over every frame.

## Timing (65 s)

| Time | Shot | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7 | Night yard, MHE-07 drives to the open door; the GPS ring breaks | Outside, GPS can find your forklift. / Inside, it can't. |
| 7–11 | Inside: the fleet at work, a "?" over every forklift | 8 forklifts. Where is each one right now? |
| 11–14 | A rack end takes a knock; the forklift backs away | A rack gets hit. Nobody knows who. |
| 14–22 | Macro: the LiDAR and the OmniBox wake up; rack focus | One LiDAR sensor. On the forklift. / No tags. Nothing on the walls. |
| 22–31 | The dot world: the radar paints the warehouse in dots | It maps your warehouse with laser light. |
| 31–36 | The orange ring closes on MHE-07; live X/Y readout | It knows where the forklift is. Within 1 cm. |
| 36–45 | MHE-07 leaves an orange line; the camera rises to all eight lines | Every move leaves a line. / Every forklift. Live. |
| 45–55 | Top view: the shift's lines weave into the heat map; call-outs | A whole shift. One picture. / See where your forklifts get stuck. |
| 55–60.5 | The map becomes the RAMS Digital Twin app | Live on your RAMS Digital Twin. |
| 60.5–65 | End card | Start with one aisle. / RAMS DIGITAL · Book a demo |
