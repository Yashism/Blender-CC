# W01 "One Weak Rack": the photoreal Blender build

Week 1 of the rack-inspection calendar (`content-plan/`). A 50-second hero film: a reach truck's
fork tip puts a 4 mm crease in the foot of an upright, nobody reports it, weeks of knocks follow,
the last pallet of the night goes up, and five bays come down into the aisle in slow motion.
Then time rewinds to the dent: a RAMS QR scan, a RED tag, the digital twin, the offer.

Everything is built from Python (Blender 4.5 LTS, Cycles). No downloaded models or textures:
every bitmap is generated by `scripts/w01/make_textures.py`, every material is procedural.

## The cut (24 fps, 65.4 s): script v2

The full script, with the reasons for each change, is in `docs/W01_SCRIPT_V2.md`. v2 is written
for a viewer who has never seen a pallet rack and is watching on mute.

| Time | Shot | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 6.0 | `s01_impact` | Macro. The fork blade drives into the upright's front bend; paint chips burst, the crease springs in. *"One night, a reach truck clips an upright."* |

| 18.0 | `s04m_lapse` | Time-lapse macro on the crease, day 1 to 47: each knock lands (fork against the steel, a jolt) and the bend deepens, DENT 4.0 → 7.4 mm; IMPACTS 1 → 7, REPORTED 0; "9 out of 10 rack impacts are never reported." |
| 24.0 | `s05_last` | *"Day 47. The last pallet of the night."* |
| 28.0 | `s06a/b/c` | The fold; bays 2 to 6 come down in slow motion; cartons rain toward the lens |
| 38.9 | `s07_freeze` + hold | *"One weak rack. Five bays down."* then *"The warning was there on day one."* |
| 43.4 | rewind | Everything backwards, DAY 47 → DAY 1, to the fresh dent |
| 46.9 | `s09_tag` | *"This is where RAMS comes in."* RAMS QR scan, the RED tag, the EN 15635 Green/Amber/Red legend |

Labels, the dent ring and the load path are pinned to the steel with camera tracks:
`blender -b blender/w01_set.blend -P scripts/w01/shots.py -- --shot s03_reveal --track` writes
`renders/w01/track/s03_reveal.json` (world points projected through the shot camera per frame).

## How it is built

| Piece | File |
| --- | --- |
| Dimensions (the single source of truth) | `scripts/w01/layout.py` |
| Generated bitmaps: slot masks, RAMS QR plate (scans to the rack-audit page), RED tag, carton print, labels, floor wear | `scripts/w01/make_textures.py` → `assets/textures/w01/` |
| Photoreal materials (powder-coat with chips/scuffs/grime/dust, galvanised, slab, kraft, pine, stretch film, fork steel) | `scripts/lib/realmats.py` |
| Racking: omega uprights (real teardrop holes and the crease shape key on the hero upright), beams, connectors, lock pins, bracing, base plates, anchors | `scripts/builders/rack_kit.py` |
| Pallet loads, reach truck, building, high-bays, compositor look | `scripts/builders/loads.py`, `reach_truck.py`, `warehouse_hero.py` |
| Set assembly (~480 loads, 4 rows) | `scripts/w01/build_set.py` → `blender/w01_set.blend` |
| Collapse: kinematic frames, breakable beam hooks, 1,288 cartons + 59 pallets as rigid bodies, volumetric dust | `scripts/w01/collapse.py` → `blender/w01_collapse.blend` |
| Shots (cameras, animation, particles, twin pass) | `scripts/w01/shots.py` |
| Edit, temp sound design, music edit, grade/HUD/captions/end card | `scripts/w01/edit.py`, `sfx.py`, `beat_edit.py`, `post.py` |

## Rebuild and render

```bash
python3 scripts/w01/make_textures.py            # only if you change a bitmap (they are committed)
blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/w01/build_set.py -- --save blender/w01_set.blend
blender -b blender/w01_set.blend -P scripts/w01/shots.py -- --shot collapse_bake

# preview quality on CPU (what the cloud container rendered): 960x540, 24 samples
bash scripts/w01/render_queue.sh 960x540 24
for s in s00_establish s02_dent s03_reveal s04m_lapse; do      # camera tracks for the pinned graphics
  blender -b blender/w01_set.blend -P scripts/w01/shots.py -- --shot $s --track; done

python3 scripts/w01/score.py                     # the orchestral score (see Music)
python3 scripts/w01/sfx.py
python3 scripts/w01/post.py                      # -> renders/w01/W01_one_weak_rack_16x9.mp4 + _9x16.mp4
```

**Final 1080p:** `kaggle/w01_final_render.ipynb` renders all 12 shots at 1920x1080, 64 samples on
Kaggle's free 2x T4 GPUs and runs the post (resumable across sessions). On a laptop GPU, add
`--device gpu --res 1920x1080 --samples 64` to the `shots.py` calls (the queue script takes the
resolution and samples as arguments), then `python3 scripts/w01/post.py --src 1920x1080`.

One shot can be re-rendered on its own, e.g.
`blender -b blender/w01_collapse.blend -P scripts/w01/shots.py -- --shot s06b_wide --res 1920x1080 --samples 64 --device gpu`.
Existing frames are skipped, so deleting a frame and re-running re-renders only that frame.

## Music

The music is the orchestral score (`scripts/w01/score.py`, below). The trap-beat versions are
kept as an alternative: `python3 scripts/w01/sfx.py --music ovo` (or nav3, mafia, paranoia,
carti) after `beat_edit.py`.

### Beat alternative

A produced trap / hip-hop beat, re-edited to the picture by `scripts/w01/beat_edit.py`
on the track's own beat grid (whole-number tempo fitted over the whole track, drops found where the
808 and kick come in):

| Time | Music |
| --- | --- |
| 1.6 | the fork hit starts the track's intro, low-passed and opening up |
| 7.0 | **drop** on the reveal; the beat runs through the time-lapse |
| 18.0 | low-pass breakdown, noise riser and a reverse-reverb swell under the last pallet |
| 22.0 | silence for the fold, then the **drop** on the first clang (22.3), on the track's hardest 10 s |
| 32.9 | tape-stop as the picture freezes, then silence |
| 34.9 | the whole cue played backwards, accelerating like the picture's rewind |
| 38.4 | melody only (a drums-out breakdown, or the intro), filter opening, while RAMS tags the upright |
| 45.4 | the beat drops back on the end card and stops on a final hit with an echo tail |

`sfx.py` mixes it with the sound design: the beat at -14 LUFS, the effects 3 LU under it, the music
ducking up to 6 dB under the big hits, -1 dBFS peak ceiling.

```bash
bash scripts/w01/fetch_music.sh               # the beats -> assets/music/w01/ (not committed)
```

### Orchestral score (default)

`scripts/w01/score.py` renders the score from real orchestral recordings: the Versilian Studios
**VSCO-2 Community Edition** library (CC0 1.0, public domain, free for commercial use, no
attribution required). String sections (sustain, spiccato, tremolo), contrabass, F horn, tenor
trombone, tuba, timpani, bass drum, crash, upright piano, harp and glockenspiel.

```bash
git clone --filter=blob:none --no-checkout --depth 1 https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE /opt/samples/VSCO-2-CE
cd /opt/samples/VSCO-2-CE && git sparse-checkout init --no-cone
printf '%s\n' /LICENSE '/Strings/Cello Section/' '/Strings/Viola Section/' '/Strings/Violin Section/' \
  '/Strings/Solo Contrabass/' '/Strings/Harp/' '/Brass/F Horn/' '/Brass/Tenor Trombone/' '/Brass/Tuba/' \
  '/Percussion/Timpani/' '/Percussion/BDrumNewhit*' '/Percussion/cymbal*' '/Percussion/Glock/' \
  '/Keys/Upright Piano/' > .git/info/sparse-checkout && git checkout master       # about 1.9 GB
pip install soundfile scipy
python3 scripts/w01/score.py      # renders/w01/w01_score.wav
python3 scripts/w01/sfx.py        # mixes the score under the sound effects -> w01_audio.wav
```

The cue (D minor, 120 bpm), cued from the edit's tags so it follows any re-cut: a held high A
over a low D under the opening aisle, with a low D minor swell and a harp note as each part of
the rack is named; timpani, bass drum and low brass on the fork's contact frame; a lone piano
question at the dent; a spiccato string ostinato that builds under the reveal and runs
i-VI-iv-V through the time-lapse, with a timpani and trombone sting on every unreported knock;
tremolo suspense under the last pallet; tutti hits on the fold and the slam with a descending
brass line; silence and one high note on the freeze, then the dent's piano question again under
"the warning was there on day one"; the collapse music reversed for the rewind; D major and the
piano motif answered when RAMS arrives; a harp sweep and rising horns as the repaired aisle
appears; a three-note sonic logo (D-A-F#, glockenspiel and piano) on the end card. VSCO-2 labels
its strings, brass and glock one octave low; `score.py` corrects that (measured against every
instrument).

## Accuracy notes for the RAMS engineering review

- The crease is 4 mm deep at the bend (`layout.DENT_DEPTH`); the time-lapse deepens it in six
  knocks (shape key 1.0 → 1.84, about 7 mm), shown as exaggerated progression for the story.
- The collapse is a dramatisation: frames are keyed to lean onto row B (17-20 degrees), beams
  are held by breakable hooks, loads are fully simulated. No people are in the aisle when it
  falls; the truck has left.
- The RED tag text follows the EN 15635 RED action (offload immediately, do not reload until
  repaired) and reads "Base crease 4 mm · Replace upright".
- The QR plate's code is real: it opens `https://rams.digital/rack-audit?asset=A-04-U05`.
- Product names on cartons (SURYA, NIRMAL, KAVERI, ORBIT, TEJAS, ANAND) are invented.
- v2 copy: "9 out of 10 rack impacts are never reported" is RAMS's own claim (rams.digital,
  "90% of impacts go unreported in Indian warehouses") and is sourced on screen; "up to 1 tonne
  each" is a typical pallet rating, not this warehouse's; the Green/Amber/Red legend follows
  RAMS's EN 15635 explainer.
