# W01 "One Weak Rack": script v2

**Why v2:** v1 assumed the viewer already knew what a pallet rack is, why a dent at the foot of an
upright matters, why the film runs backwards, and who RAMS is. A newcomer on mute got a
beautiful collapse and no story. v2 keeps every v1 shot and the orchestral score, and adds what
a first-time viewer is missing:

1. **Where we are:** an opening wide down the aisle that names the parts (pallet, beam, upright).
2. **Why a 4 mm dent matters:** the load drawn as light running down the upright into the dent.
3. **That nobody reported it:** impact counter vs. "reported 0", plus RAMS's 9-in-10 statistic.
4. **Why it rewinds:** "The warning was there on day one", then a day counter running back.
5. **What RAMS did:** inspection, the EN 15635 RED class explained, the twin, the repaired aisle.
6. **What to do now:** "Catch it on day one", then the audit offer.

Runtime 61.9 s (v1: 50.4 s). 16:9 and 9:16. Every line is on screen, so it works on mute; no VO.

## Claims to confirm with RAMS before publishing

- **"9 out of 10 rack impacts are never reported"**: RAMS's own claim on rams.digital ("90% of
  impacts go unreported in Indian warehouses"). The source line says so on screen.
- **"Up to 1 tonne each"** per pallet: a typical rating, not this warehouse's data.
- **4 mm dent tagged RED**: RAMS engineers should confirm a base crease of this depth is RED for
  the upright profile shown (see Accuracy notes in `W01_ONE_WEAK_RACK.md`).
- **EN 15635 legend wording**: Green = monitor, Amber = repair within 4 weeks, Red = unload now
  (from RAMS's EN 15635 explainer).
